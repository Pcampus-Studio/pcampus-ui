## Context

Experience Studio UAT showed consumers wrapping `AppShell` with `max-w-3xl`, `p-8`, and a flat canvas. The workbench look (full-width main, `p-4`, dark canvas + token glow) must live in `@pcampus-studio/ui` so every new Pcampus service gets it without a local shell.

## Intent

**Why:** If the default stays a padded article column, every consumer re-skins AppShell and the ecosystem drifts.

**Done when:** A new app that mounts `ThemeProvider` + `AppShell` without extra layout classes gets a full-width `p-4` main, token atmosphere, and dark theme. Existing services may pass `theme="light"` or `theme="system"` and `mainClassName` to opt out.

**Unblocks:** Experience Studio and future services stop owning shell padding, width, and atmosphere.
